Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 7, 2025  ·  Tonnage

Introduction

Tonnage ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile application and related services.

By using Tonnage, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, do not use the app.

Information we collect

Account and authentication: email address and password if you register with email; when you sign in with Google, we receive identifiers Google provides for that sign-in (typically your email address, and sometimes your name), processed by our authentication provider.

Profile and preferences: training goal, experience level, optional body weight, onboarding status, and subscription or trial dates where applicable.

Workout data: routines, exercises, workout sessions (start and end times), optional session notes, and set logs (weight, repetitions, set type, confirmation, timestamps).

Analytics: product events (e.g. sign-up, workouts started or completed, sharing, navigation), screen names, and association with your user ID when logged in; device and app technical data as collected by our analytics SDK.

Local device storage: queued or cached workout data for offline use until it syncs to our servers.

How we use information

We use the information above to provide and improve the service, authenticate you, sync your data, show your training history, and understand how the product is used.

We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

We use third-party services that process data on our behalf, including hosting and authentication (Supabase), sign-in with Google (Google), and analytics (PostHog). Their use of data is governed by their respective privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

We retain your information while your account exists and as needed to operate the service. You may request account deletion through in-app options where available; some data may be retained as required by law or for legitimate business purposes.

Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

Tonnage is not directed at children under the age where parental consent is required in your region. We do not knowingly collect personal information from such children.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version in the app and may notify you as required by law.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us using the details provided in the app store listing or your account correspondence channel.
